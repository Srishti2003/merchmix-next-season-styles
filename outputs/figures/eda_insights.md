# EDA insights

- **Strong seasonality.** Weekly units range from 177k (week of 04 Dec 2019) to 636k (week of 19 Jun 2019), a 3.6× swing; the last 8 weeks are +1% vs the same 8 weeks a year earlier.
- **Long tail.** In the last 52 weeks 32,999 styles sold at least once; the top 1% (329 styles) took 31% of units and just 961 styles (2.9%) made half of all units.
- **Short lifecycles.** For styles launched inside the window, the median selling span is 31 weeks (median 19 weeks with any sale); 23% are done within 12 weeks.
- **Newness drives volume.** On average 32% of weekly units come from styles first sold < 12 weeks earlier (range 20%–46%) — history-only models will miss much of next period's demand.
- **Mix shifts with season.** Summer vs winter, Ladieswear gains +4.9% pts of unit share and Baby/Children loses -3.2% pts; online share of units moved from 69% (2019) to 73% (2020, COVID).
